Healthcare Management System Status: Academic / Personal Project

Online Queue Management System for Hospital Management

An online queue management system designed to improve hospital patient flow through digital registration, appointment scheduling, queue monitoring, and attendance management. The system provides patients with electronic queue numbers and real-time queue visibility while helping doctors and administrators manage appointments and patient flow.

Problem

Overcrowded waiting rooms, manual token dispatch confusion, lack of patient queue visibility, and inefficient appointment handling across departments.

Objective

Streamline outpatient transit, automate token numbers, deliver live waiting status, and centralize records for doctor and admin operations.

Key Features:
• Patient registration and login
• Online appointment booking
• Automatic token/queue number generation
• Patient check-in and attendance
• Real-time queue tracking
• Doctor dashboard
• Admin dashboard
• Notifications/reminders
• Patient and appointment management
• Reports and analytics
Smart Healthcare + Traffic Management Status: Academic / Personal Project

Smart Ambulance Traffic Clearance & Emergency Coordination System

A web-based healthcare and smart-traffic management system designed to reduce delays faced by ambulances due to traffic congestion. The system connects ambulances, traffic police, and hospitals through a common real-time platform.

Core Innovation

"Instead of waiting until an ambulance reaches a junction before responding, the system aims to alert traffic authorities in advance so upcoming junctions can be prepared before the ambulance arrives."
